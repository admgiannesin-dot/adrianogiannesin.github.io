Biography

Adriano Giannesin is a Brazilian author whose debut novel, Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle, was written and published simultaneously in Portuguese, English, and Spanish — a singular act that places the novel itself inside the territory it explores: the crossing of borders that language, grief, and memory all resist.

His work occupies the space between what science can preserve and what only a human life can hold. The fiction is grounded in character, driven by grief, and anchored in the conviction that some stories must be told in more than one tongue — that translation is not a concession to the reader, but an original act of meaning.

Mnemosyne draws on the Greek myth of Orpheus and Eurydice not as allegory but as deep structure: a shape that the narrative inhabits without announcing. The result is a literary novel that works as a standalone experience even for readers who have never encountered the myth.

Giannesin is based in Brazil. Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle is Book 1 of The Orpheus Cycle series.
