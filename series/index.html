Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle

The Orpheus Cycle · 2026

Henry Wallace lost the two people he loved most. First Emily. Then Clara. While working at the Atlas Complex, Henry discovers a place that shouldn't exist: a hidden library beneath the complex, preserved by time and wrapped in a silence that defies any explanation. The encounter that happens there transforms everything he thought he knew about memory, loss, and reality itself.

A novel about love, grief, and the power of memories — those that stay with us, change us, and sometimes refuse to disappear.

The Myth

Orpheus and the Shape of the Series

The Orpheus Cycle uses the Greek myth of Orpheus and Eurydice not as allegory, but as deep structure — a shape that the narrative inhabits without announcing. The myth is present in the architecture of the story, not on its surface.

In the myth, Orpheus descends to retrieve what he has lost and is given a single condition he cannot fulfill. The series explores what happens after that moment: what grief makes of us when looking back is the only thing we know how to do.

About the Author

Adriano Giannesin is a Brazilian author writing in Portuguese, English, and Spanish. The Orpheus Cycle is his debut series, published simultaneously in three languages in 2026.
