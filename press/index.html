About the book

Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle

Henry Wallace lost the two people he loved most. First Emily. Then Clara.

While working at the Atlas Complex, Henry discovers a place that shouldn't exist: a hidden library beneath the complex, preserved by time and wrapped in a silence that defies any explanation. The encounter that happens there transforms everything he thought he knew about memory, loss, and reality itself.

Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle is a novel about love, grief, and the power of memories — those that stay with us, change us, and sometimes refuse to disappear.

About the author

Adriano Giannesin

Adriano Giannesin is a Brazilian author writing in Portuguese, English, and Spanish. His debut novel, Mnemosyne: The Orpheus Cycle, was written and published simultaneously in three languages — a decision that places the novel itself inside the territory it explores: the crossing of borders that language, grief, and memory all resist.

His work occupies the space between what science can preserve and what only a human life can hold. The fiction is grounded in character, driven by grief, and anchored in the conviction that some stories must be told in more than one tongue.

Mnemosyne is Book 1 of The Orpheus Cycle series.
